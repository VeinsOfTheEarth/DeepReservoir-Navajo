# Objective Locations Schematic

This figure combines the operator-informed objective priorities with a
nongeographic operational schematic of Navajo Reservoir and the San Juan
River. It intentionally treats the priority levels as design guidance rather
than a hard optimization hierarchy.

Storage is shown separately as a model-enabling objective because it was not
stated as an independent operator objective, but the policy requires carryover
storage to sustain the other objectives. NIIP is represented only by its annual
agricultural delivery-volume objective; daily delivery timing is excluded.

## Authoritative Source

`objective-locations-schematic.pptx` is the manually refined source, renamed
from `objective-locations-schematic-hand-edit.pptx` without changing its contents.
`objective-locations-schematic.pdf` is the matching final paper export. Edit
the PowerPoint and export a new PDF for revisions.

## Original Generators

`build.py` and `export-editable-powerpoint.ps1` are retained as provenance for
the original programmatic handoff, not as reproductions of the final manual
layout. The Python builder writes PNG/PDF/SVG beside itself and would overwrite
the final PDF; do not run it over the final exports. The paper-wide rebuild
command deliberately skips this directory.

The earlier `objective-locations-schematic-editable.pptx`, PNG/SVG previews,
and alternative layout mockups are in the ignored local archive
`.figure-archive/repo-extras-2026-09-08/paper/figures/objective-locations-schematic/`.

The schematic's objective palette is the approved paper-wide standard:
charcoal dam safety, deep-red flood safety, amber hydropower, royal-blue
storage, teal ESA minimum flow, green NIIP, and purple SPR. The programmatic
figures use the matching colors from `paper/figure-support/figurestyle.py`.
