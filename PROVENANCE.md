# Public snapshot provenance

This repository was assembled on September 23, 2026 as a fresh public history from the project's corrected recovery code. Private Git history, development worktrees, local experiment directories, caches, and unrelated research material were not imported.

The snapshot includes the corrected physical environment and metrics, the complete 128-policy Phase 95 search, and the selected task-105 checkpoint with its daily evaluation, training trace, stress tests, and rebuilt paper outputs. Source identifiers in the manifests bind these artifacts to recovery commit `8e7fdcf7f68a103b7249dfb8516b8a28dce87f62` and plan `a46c01d4fdda860feb330e4af867ce73a003880c6a73745e16d9e466d6e8b009`.

Appendix packages were ported from the manuscript working repository and made repository-relative. External reports and historical slide decks that are not runtime dependencies are linked or accepted as optional provenance inputs instead of being redistributed.
